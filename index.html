Clear scan activity

VJ01 / master access only. Paste the order numbers whose scan activity should be wiped for a date (any text is fine — 9-digit order numbers are picked out). Each order stays on that day's manifest but goes back to pending, with who scanned/delivered it, the times and the trolley/scan counts all cleared, so it no longer shows in the Activity Log, Dashboard or reports. Preloaded orders are skipped. This can't be undone.

Safety copies

A copy of a date’s data is saved automatically just before it is re-uploaded, deleted or has scans cleared (the latest 40 are kept, and copies older than the 30-day retention are dropped). Restoring replaces that date’s current data with the copy — the current data is saved as a safety copy first. VJ01 / master access only.
